# Data Access: IMpower150 Clinical and ctDNA Study

This repository contains information regarding the access and availability of clinical and circulating tumor DNA (ctDNA) data for the **IMpower150** clinical trial.

## Data Repository
All clinical and ctDNA data for IMpower150 are deposited to the **European Genome-Phenome Archive (EGA)**.

* **Accession Number:** [EGAS00001006703](https://ega-archive.org/studies/EGAS00001006703)

## Requesting Patient-Level Data
Qualified researchers may request access to individual patient-level data through the clinical study data request platform, **Vivli**.

* **Platform:** [https://vivli.org/](https://vivli.org/)
* **Roche Membership & Criteria:** Details on Roche’s criteria for eligible studies can be found at [Vivli Member Page](https://vivli.org/members/ourmembers).

## Roche Data Sharing Policy
For further details on Roche’s Global Policy regarding the sharing of clinical information and instructions on how to request access to related clinical study documents (CSRs, protocols, etc.), please refer to the official Roche commitment page:

* **Policy Link:** [Roche Commitment to Data Sharing](https://www.roche.com/research_and_development/who_we_are_how_we_work/clinical_trials/our_commitment_to_data_sharing.htm)
